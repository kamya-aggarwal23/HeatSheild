## Analysis Record 01 — Historical Weather Patterns

**Objective:** Understand Tokyo's historical weather behaviour and identify patterns relevant to daily maximum-temperature prediction.

**Analysis performed:**
- Studied monthly temperature averages.
- Examined seasonal temperature variations.
- Investigated humidity and rainfall patterns.
- Examined historical extreme-heat occurrences.

**Observations:**
- Strong seasonal temperature variation.
- Highest average maximum temperature occurs in August.
- Summer months experience high humidity.
- Extreme-heat occurrences have increased in recent years.

**Outcome:**
- Identified seasonal variation as an important consideration.
- Identified humidity as a potential future model feature.
- Established the need to investigate historical extreme-heat frequency.

**Impact on project:**
- Retain the 8-day temperature lag as the V1 baseline.
- Consider seasonal features for V1.1.
- Keep humidity outside initial V1 scope.

**Sources:**
- JMA Climate Normals
- JMA Historical Weather Records

**Status:** Initial research completed; findings pending verification.
