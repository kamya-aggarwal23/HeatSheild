
# HeatShield V1 — Project Scope

## Objective
Predict the next day's maximum temperature in Tokyo
using historical daily weather data.

## Primary Goal
Help people anticipate tomorrow's heat conditions
and plan their daily activities.

## Input
Historical daily weather observations available
up to the current prediction date.

## Output
1. Predicted maximum temperature (°C).
2. Heat severity classification.

## Classification
- Below 25°C: Normal
- 25–29.9°C: Warm
- 30–34.9°C: Hot
- 35°C and above: Extremely Hot

## V1 Limitations
- Daily predictions only.
- Tokyo location only.
- No hourly predictions.
- No humidity-based heat index.
- No advanced safety recommendations.

## Success Criteria
A reliable next-day temperature prediction
evaluated on unseen future dates.
